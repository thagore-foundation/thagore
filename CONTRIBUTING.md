# CONTRIBUTING.md

## 1. Zero-Tolerance Policy: AI-Generated Content

To maintain the integrity of the **Thagore** programming language, we enforce a **Human-Only contribution policy**.

* **Prohibition:** The use of autonomous agents, including but not limited to **OpenClaw**, Moltbot, or large language models (LLMs) to generate Issues, Pull Requests, or Forum posts is strictly forbidden without express written consent from the Thagore Foundation.
* **Identification:** Submissions that exhibit patterns of automated generation (e.g., lack of specific context, generic descriptions, or headers typical of agent output) will be closed and deleted without warning.

---

## 2. The "Human-Proof" Submission Gate

To prove you are a human and have read these terms, **every Issue and Pull Request MUST include the following "Verification Header" at the very top of the description:**

> **[HUMAN-VERIFIED: THAGORE-PROTECT-2026]**
> *I certify that this contribution was authored by a human and I accept the Thagore Contribution License.*

**Failure to include this exact string will result in the immediate, automated closure of your submission.**

---

## 3. Maintainer Discretion & Right to Refuse

By contributing to this project, you acknowledge and agree to the following legal protections for the Thagore Foundation:

* **Absolute Discretion:** Maintainers reserve the absolute right to close, lock, or delete any Issue or Pull Request for any reason, or no reason at all, without prior consultation or request.
* **No Obligation to Review:** Opening a Pull Request does not create a contract or obligation for the Thagore Foundation to provide a code review or feedback.
* **Spam Classification:** Any automated or bulk-generated content is classified as "Network Interference." We reserve the right to report the associated GitHub accounts for Terms of Service violations.

## 5. How to Contribute (The Human Way)

1. **Search First:** Ensure your issue hasn't been reported.
2. **Rust Standards:** All code must pass `cargo fmt` and `cargo clippy`.
3. **The Header:** Include the `[HUMAN-VERIFIED]` string mentioned in Section 2.
4. **The "Why":** Explain the technical necessity of your change in plain language.
